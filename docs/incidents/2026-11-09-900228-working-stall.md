## Root cause

### 1. Why a dead or hung card can stay `working`, and why nobody saw it (#900228)

How the poller decides `working`: `getSessionState` reports `working` as long as the
session's tmux session or jail container exists and the transcript's last event is not a
`result`. Ruling things out against the code:

- **Never spawned: ruled out as the cause of a 40-minute `working`.** Every start route
  writes `status='working'` only after `startSession` returns a session id. A spawn that
  throws (no seat, lease refused, clone failed) never reaches `working`.
- **Died while the card stayed `working`: ruled out for 40 minutes.** If the process is
  gone, the poller's dead branch routes the card to review within one 3-second tick.
- **Held by the session cap, queue or a lock: ruled out.** `startSession` throws before
  the status write, and a queued follow-up that later gets a `result` routes to review.
- **What is left:** the process **spawned and then hung**, either before its first model
  event (auth, model call, git or worktree setup, a lock wait inside the wrapper) or
  between events. tmux or the jail was alive and no `result` was written, so the poller
  said `working`, which is exactly what it is built to say.

The missing piece was **detection**. Nothing turned "alive but silent" into a signal:

- `objectives.last_activity_at` was written **only when a session ended**
  (`session-intel-pipeline.ts`), so a bot reading the card could not see "nothing happened
  for 25 minutes".
- The only checks were a 30-minute `session_stuck` WebSocket broadcast (UI only: no DB
  row, no `objective_events`, no bot wake) and the 90-minute idle force-route to review.
  At 40 minutes neither had done anything a bot could see.

Plainly: **the session process started and then hung, and the server had no check that
turns "working but silent" into an alert. The only backstops were a UI-only alert at
30 minutes and a forced move at 90.** I could not tell which step it hung on (auth,
model call, git or a lock) because #900228's transcript and logs are outside what this
session can read. The watchdog now records that as `stall_reason` (`hung_startup` vs
`hung_no_heartbeat`, plus phase `no_cli_output` / `init_only` / `model_active`), so the
next occurrence will name its own step.

### 2. Why session accounting and the timeline go missing (#900371)

`session_count`, `total_tokens`, `total_cost_usd`, `last_activity_at` and the
`session_intel` timeline row are all written from one place: the extraction queued by
`handleSessionDeath`. Two paths skipped it:

- **(a) A card moved to `review` before its session ended was never accounted.** The poller
  skips `review` cards (`if (objective.status === 'review') continue`) and calls
  `handleSessionDeath` only on its own `working → review` transition. The per-tick review
  sweep then **NULLed `session_id` without accounting the run**. Anything that moves a card
  to review while the worker is still running loses that run: the worker or a bot
  PATCHing the status, a gate, or a stop. With `session_id` gone, `/output` has nothing to
  read, so the timeline is empty. #900371 matches this exactly: `status=review`,
  `session_id=null`, `session_count=0`, `/output` empty. Its single session clearly ran:
  it wrote a progress event and opened PR #670.
- **(b) A session that ended after a backend restart was never accounted.**
  `handleSessionDeath` returned immediately for any session missing from the in-memory
  `activeSessions` map, and every self-deploy or restart wipes that map while tmux and the
  jails keep running. Self-deploys are frequent (auto-deploy runs on every merged PR;
  #667 merged 09:25 ET and #670 merged 10:26 ET today).
- **(c) The transcript was then deleted.** In jail mode the canonical transcript is a
  symlink into the jail home. Normal death handling copies the file out first, but the
  orphan jail-home sweep did not, so sessions that hit (a) or (b) lost their transcript
  when the sweep ran.

**Consequence for the watchdog:** `session_count`, `total_tokens` and `last_activity_at`
cannot be used as liveness signals, because they are 0 or null on a card that clearly did
work. The watchdog judges a card only on **process liveness plus transcript write time and
content**, and it maintains `last_activity_at` itself as a live heartbeat.

  **1** card (#900371) lost its session accounting through root cause 2.
- **your-org and other workspaces: not measurable from this sandbox (403).** The
  per-event timestamps in `/output` are parse-time, not event-time, so "10+ minutes with no
  events" cannot be rebuilt through the API anyway. Count across the whole board on the host:

  ```sql
  -- working cards that were restarted after ≥10 min with no activity on the previous session
  SELECT s1.objective_id, s1.session_id, s1.created_at AS started, s2.created_at AS restarted, s2.reason
    FROM session_starts s1
    JOIN session_starts s2 ON s2.objective_id = s1.objective_id AND s2.id > s1.id
                          AND s2.kind IN ('spawn','resume') AND s2.session_id != s1.session_id
   WHERE s1.created_at >= strftime('%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%fZ','now','-7 days')
     AND s1.kind IN ('spawn','resume')
     AND (julianday(s2.created_at) - julianday(s1.created_at)) * 1440 >= 10
     AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM session_intel i WHERE i.session_id = s1.session_id AND i.tool_calls > 0)
     AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM session_starts m WHERE m.objective_id = s1.objective_id AND m.id > s1.id AND m.id < s2.id);
  ```

  Once this ships, the count is direct: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM objective_events WHERE kind='stalled'`
  plus `activity_log` rows with `metadata.kind = 'stall_auto_restart'`.

## Fix (this PR)

1. **Stall watchdog** (`services/stall-watchdog.ts`). Runs on a 60-second tick inside the
   poller.
   - Keeps `last_activity_at` up to date as a live heartbeat (forward-only).
   - Classifies a `working` card as `never_spawned`, `died_silently`, `hung_startup` or
     `hung_no_heartbeat` once it has been silent for **N minutes** (`stall_watchdog_minutes`,
     default **10**; env `CC_STALL_WATCHDOG_MINUTES` overrides). A tool call still waiting
     for its result gets `stall_watchdog_inflight_minutes` (default 30).
   - Cards with a pending wakeup, a 529 retry, an arena cohort or `delegate_mode` are exempt.
   - The first time a card stalls, it is **restarted automatically, once**, with the reason
     recorded: `session_starts.reason='stall_watchdog'`, an `activity_log` milestone, and
     `objectives.stall_restarts`.
   - The second stall, or a failed restart, **flags** the card instead: `stalled_at` and
     `stall_reason` are set, which inserts an `objective_events` row of kind `stalled`. That
     row feeds `/api/agent/changes` and a `needs_agent` webhook of kind `stalled` to the
     owner bot and General, and a high-severity AlertBell alert is raised.
   - The restart count resets only on a start that is NOT the watchdog's own (a person, a
     bot or a follow-up), so it can never restart in a loop. Settings can turn restarts off
     (`stall_watchdog_max_restarts=0`) but can never raise the cap above one. Kill switch:
     `stall_watchdog_enabled=0`.
2. **API.** `get_objective` / `list_objectives` (REST `mapObjective` and the MCP card row)
   carry `last_activity_at`, `stalled` (true only while the card is still `working`),
   `stalled_at` and `stall_reason`. `needs_agent` / `get_attention` gain kind `stalled`.
3. **Accounting fixes** (root cause 2):
   - The review sweep (`planReviewSessionSweep`) accounts a dead session before it clears
     `session_id`, and leaves a still-running session alone for a 15-minute grace period
     (`CC_REVIEW_SWEEP_LIVE_GRACE_MS`).
   - `handleSessionDeath` rebuilds a session missing from the map from its DB row
     (`rehydrateEndedSession`). It is idempotent: it skips sessions already handled in this
     process and sessions that already have a `session_intel` row.
   - The orphan jail-home sweep copies the canonical transcript out before deleting the
     jail home.

### Relationship to #687 (obj 900510), after rebasing onto main

#687 landed while this PR was open. It does not overlap with this PR's fixes, and the two
depend on each other, so nothing from this PR was dropped:

- #687 made the **startup sweep** call `handleSessionDeath` for a `working` card whose
  session died during a restart. On `main`, `handleSessionDeath` still returns early for a
  session missing from the in-memory map, and right after a restart every session is
  missing. So that call only records usage when `rehydrateEndedSession` (this PR) is also
  present. #687 covers `working` cards on boot; this PR also covers `review` cards and
  sessions that end on a later tick.
- #687's **zero-token review sweep** stamps `blocked` on any `review` card with 0 tokens,
  $0 and no summary. Without a guard it would fire on exactly the #900371 shape, where the
  card was moved to review mid-turn, the worker is still running, and its usage is
  recorded only when it exits. The sweep now skips a card whose session is still alive
  (`applyZeroTokenReviewBlock(db, { isSessionAlive })`). The card becomes eligible once
  the session is gone.
- #676's follow-up queue is unaffected: the watchdog does not count queued messages as
  activity. A resume through `handleQueuedFollowUps` is a non-watchdog start, so it
  re-arms the one-restart cap, the same as any other new turn.

- #671's accountability record (merged after this rebase) has its own `stalled`
  *accountability state*. It is inferred from `last_meaningful_progress_at` and is
  declared-first and default-off. This PR's `stalled` flag is something different: a
  process and heartbeat signal on a `working` card, stored in `stalled_at`. They live in
  different columns and API fields, and neither writes the other.

**Reuse (#900547).** `restartStalled` and `flagStalled` are exported. `restartStalled`
enforces the one-attempt cap itself, through a compare-and-set on `stall_restarts` capped at
`DEFAULT_MAX_AUTO_RESTARTS`, so another caller cannot cause a second restart.
`flagStalled` is the alert path: it sets the stalled flag, writes the activity row, sends
the `needs_agent` notification and broadcasts the change.
